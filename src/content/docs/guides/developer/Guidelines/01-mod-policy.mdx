Innersloth has been very accepting of user-made mods for Among Us, the [Mod Policy](https://www.innersloth.com/among-us-mod-policy/) was written to keep a set of rules for mod developers to follow.

These policies can be split into **3 Categories**:

### Required:
1. The Mod Stamp must be shown on screen at all times, if your mod uses Reactor, this is already taken care of for you, otherwise, you must patch `MainMenuManager.Start` and call `ModManager.Instance.ShowModStamp();`
2. The following disclaimer must be displayed on your mod's website/GitHub repository:
``` This mod is not affiliated with Among Us or Innersloth LLC, and the content contained therein is not endorsed or otherwise sponsored by Innersloth LLC. Portions of the materials contained herein are property of Innersloth LLC. © Innersloth LLC.```
3. Any mod that changes the game flow on official servers (through adding roles, or modifying interactions) must register itself during the lobby creation sequence, client side mods are exempt from this. More info can be found [here](https://github.com/Innersloth-LLC/AmongUsModdingInformation).

### Discouraged:
1. Any form of paywalling/selling of mods is discouraged rather than being prohibited, Innersloth has clearly stated that they want mod developers to get paid, but would prefer that mods be free.

### Prohibited:
1. Creating cheat mods that offer an unfair advantage over other players.
2. Distributing malware disguised as a mod.
3. Creating clones of Among Us in any capacity.
4. Integrating the Among Us IP into other games.
5. Don’t use the Among Us IP in an inappropriate, defamatory way.
6. Don't distribute the game's files alongside your mod.
7. Don't state or imply that your mod is endorsed by Innersloth.

## Mod Categorization:

Innersloth classifies mods under **3 categories**:

### All Clients Mods:

These are mods that are **required** for everyone to play.

### Host Only Mods:

These are mods that are **only required on the host's client**, meaning unmodded clients can join and play without installing the mod.
These are generally much more limited than all clients mods.

### Client Side Mods:

These are mods which make changes on **your local client**, you can freely use these without others needing to install them.

:::note
If you are using Reactor, you can register your mods using the`[ReactorModFlags]` attribute in your plugin class.
- All Clients: use `ModFlags.RequireOnAllClients`.
- Host Only: use `ModFlags.DisableServerAuthority`.
- Client Side; use `ModFlags.None`.
:::

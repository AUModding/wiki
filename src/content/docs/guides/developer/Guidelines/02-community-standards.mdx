title: Community Standards

---
The Among Us modding community generally shares a set of standards regarding mods:

1. Although AI assistance is acceptable to some degree, fully **vibecoded** mods are heavily frowned upon.
2. **AI Art in mods** is despised by players, as it actively inhibits creativity.
3. It is preferred that mods be **open source and unobsfuscated.**
4. **P2W Features** are hated by players, as they financially exploit users in exchange for an unfair advantage, these are treated **on the same level as cheats.**
5. **Paywalled mods** are frowned upon by users, mods should be free to use by anyone.

:::note
These community standards aren't fully set in stone, but it is preferred you follow them to avoid **negative reception from users.**
:::
